Seminars

At Oxford, I was a co-organizer (with Guillem Cazassus, Shih-Kai Chiu, and Ilyas Khan) of the Oxford Geometry and Analysis seminar.

Previously, at Columbia, I (co-)organized graduate student seminar series on various topics in mathematical physics, enumerative geometry, and representation theory. This series has since been continued by various people.

  • (Spring 2021) Modern techniques in representation theory (with Cailan Li and Mrudul Thatte)
  • (Summer/Fall 2020) Things I should know about category O but I don't (with Cailan Li and Mrudul Thatte)
  • (Fall 2019) Learning seminar on conformal field theory
  • (Spring 2019) Learning seminar on symplectic duality
  • (Fall 2018) Enumerative geometry seminar on the GW/DT correspondence (with Melissa Liu)
  • (Fall 2017) Student enumerative geometry seminar on topics in curve counting (with Noah Arbesfeld, Petr Pushkar and Shuai Wang)

In Spring 2019, I also co-organized the Columbia Enumerative geometry seminar (with Melissa Liu).
