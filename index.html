Hello World!

I am currently at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center (YMSC) at Tsinghua University as an Assistant Professor.

From 2023-2026, I was a Project Researcher at Kavli IPMU. From 2021-2023, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Mathematical Institute of the University of Oxford, working with Dominic Joyce.

I finished my PhD in mathematics in May 2021 at Columbia University. where my advisor was Andrei Okounkov. Before that, I was an undergraduate student at the University of Waterloo, where I majored in Computer Science and Pure Mathematics.

My legal name is Huaxin Liu (刘华昕), but I have gone by Henry since I was very little.
